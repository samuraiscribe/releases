# Getting help with Samurai Scribe

## Something is broken, or involves your own meetings

**Email `support@samuraiscribe.com`.** No GitHub account needed, and it is
private — which matters, because a bug report usually quotes the meeting that
broke.

The fastest route is inside the app: **Settings → Support → "Report a problem"**.
It builds a troubleshooting file (your logs, app version, OS, which models are
installed), shows it to you in Finder or Explorer, and opens an email for you to
attach it to. Nothing is sent until you send it, and it contains no meeting
content unless you pick a meeting yourself.

We usually reply within two business days.

## You want to see what is already known, or ask for something

[**Issues**](https://github.com/samuraiscribe/releases/issues) on this repo are
the public list of known problems and requested features. Please 👍 an existing
request rather than opening a duplicate — that is how we decide what to build.

Note that issues are public and permanent. Anything touching your recordings,
your licence, or a confidential meeting belongs in an email instead.

## Privacy

Samurai Scribe runs entirely on your machine.
[`docs/NETWORK.md`](https://samuraiscribe.com/network) lists every network
request the app can make, and invites you to verify it with Little Snitch or
Wireshark.
